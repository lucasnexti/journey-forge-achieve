# supabase/functions
- The Nexti na Prática AI assistant answers only from approved sources in notion_sources/notion_chunks (legacy table names; Notion retired). Provider wiki: the Nexti Wiki (Outline at wiki.shared.nexti.com) read with WIKI_API_KEY by the assistant-knowledge function (sync_wiki by admins, scheduled_sync hourly via cron secret), incremental by updatedAt, hierarchy kept as coleção › documento, removals only after a complete read, personal data redacted (wiki-text.ts); provider octadesk / manuais: prepared indexes in the private assistant-knowledge bucket (import_octadesk / import_manuais, built with scripts/assistant-octadesk and scripts/assistant-manuais). nexti-ai-chat retrieves through the service-role-only search_notion_knowledge RPC (approval, audience and module filtered) before calling the model; chats are not stored. Why: customers must never receive unapproved or internal content, and a failed sync/import must keep the previous base.
- Materials cited by the Nexti assistant (except Wiki documents, which are support-only and never sent with a title/link or shown, and Octadesk articles, which are never linked — only their linked Nexti na Prática orientation is; both enforced in nexti-ai-chat and parseAnswer) open inside Nexti na Prática (ManualViewer over the iframe, chat stays open) from notion_sources.viewer_url, which only the imports fill and which is limited by a DB check and inAppViewer() to Google Drive previews and manuais-nexti files; other sources open in a new tab. Why: Octadesk article pages render blank from the app and must not be embedded arbitrarily.
